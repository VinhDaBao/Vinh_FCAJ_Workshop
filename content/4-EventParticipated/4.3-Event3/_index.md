# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day 30/05/2026”

### Event Objectives

- Encourage students to present confidently and practice English speaking
- Create a real-world space for students and professionals to share technology knowledge
- Help participants step outside their comfort zone and build professional connections

### Speakers

- **Huỳnh Thái Linh** – Shared how to use LocalStack with AWS
- **The Boilers Team (FPT University)** – Minh Hương, Quốc An, and Trí Quân presented their hackathon experience in English
- **Nguyễn Thị Quỳnh Như (HUTECH University)** – Spoke about psychology, self-doubt, and confidence building
- **Anh Nghĩa (Swinburne)** – Shared a startup idea combining AI and astrology
- **Trần Minh Quân (FPT University)** – Talked about hidden problems in software development
- **Khắc Uyên (Vietnam-Germany University - VGU)** – Shared how to overcome procrastination and invisible fears

### Key Highlights

#### LocalStack for faster testing

- LocalStack made testing about 138 times faster than using real cloud services  
- It used very little RAM  
- A limitation is that it supports only a limited number of AWS services  

#### Hackathon projects

- The Boilers Team completed an AI fake-voice detection project for call-center fraud prevention  
- They also built a platform for creating mock interviews from CVs  

#### Self-doubt and Imposter Syndrome

- Self-doubt and imposter syndrome can make students miss opportunities  
- Many people hide their real skills because they do not feel ready yet  

#### AI and astrology project

- The startup idea combined large language models such as Claude with RAG  
- Cross-validation with Bát tự logic achieved a very high agreement rate  

#### Software iceberg problems

- Many software failures come from unclear requirements, manual workflows, and weak communication  
- The biggest issues are often hidden below the surface  

#### Procrastination and fear

- Procrastination is often a defense mechanism, not just laziness  
- Common fears include not feeling skilled enough, fear of judgment, and fear of failure  

### Key Takeaways

#### Action creates confidence

- Confidence comes from doing, not from waiting to feel ready  
- The Amazon-style "Go Build" mindset encourages fast action, early mistakes, and quick fixes  

#### Use the 5-second rule

- Start immediately when you notice hesitation  
- If needed, commit to doing one hard task for just 5 minutes to build momentum  

#### Join hackathons often

- Hackathons provide free resources, networking, and practical projects  
- Real projects make a CV stronger and more credible than theory alone  

### Event Experience

The event was highly interactive and covered both technical and soft-skill topics in a balanced way.

#### Strong audience interaction
- Speakers kept asking practical questions to break the silence and create discussion  
- The atmosphere felt open and friendly throughout the session  

#### Wide topic coverage
- The program mixed Cloud, software architecture, AI, psychology, and emotional management  
- This made the event useful for both technical and personal growth  

#### Supportive environment
- First-time speakers and nervous participants were still respected and encouraged  
- The event ended with warm appreciation and a positive feeling for everyone involved
